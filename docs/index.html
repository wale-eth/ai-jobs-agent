How this works

Straight from the source. Job boards like LinkedIn copy their listings from company hiring systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), usually hours or days later. FirstSeen polls those hiring systems directly every 30 minutes, so every link here goes to the original posting, the same page that processes applications.

Sponsorship, two ways. Each ad's text is classified into three tiers: it explicitly offers visa sponsorship, it explicitly rules sponsorship out, or it doesn't say (which is common and often still worth applying to). Separately, the ✓ licensed sponsor badge means the company itself is on the UK Home Office register of licensed Skilled Worker sponsors, so it can sponsor even when an ad is silent. Register checked 3 Aug 2026.

Detection latency is the time between a company publishing a job and this tracker spotting it. With sweeps every 30 minutes the typical catch happens within about 15 minutes of posting, which is the head start this site exists to give you.
